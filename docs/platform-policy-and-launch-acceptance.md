# Platform policy and launch acceptance

AlmostLegitTV is a booking and creator-discovery surface. It does not sell games, transfer wallet funds, issue gift codes, collect service fees, store payment details, or represent that a stream will occur. Any purchase, wallet credit, redemption, refund, regional eligibility, or platform transaction remains entirely within Xbox or PlayStation and the parties’ own platform accounts.

## PlayStation guidance

PlayStation does not provide the same native game-gifting path as Xbox for every title and region. The site therefore never presents a PlayStation wallet balance, transfer, gift-code, or purchase workflow. A viewer may suggest or request a stream for a PlayStation title; the creator reviews ownership and availability, and any decision about acquiring or playing the title is handled independently on the platform. The site records only booking state and creator-confirmed ownership state.

## Roles and boundaries

| Role | Allowed | Not allowed |
|---|---|---|
| Viewer | Browse approved profiles, submit authenticated stream requests, suggest titles, and see safe public statuses. | View private handles, edit creator profiles, approve applications, or transact. |
| Creator | Maintain the approved public profile, review private requests and suggestions, confirm ownership, and change booking statuses for the matching profile. | Self-publish an unapproved portfolio or access another creator’s private queue. |
| Administrator | Review applications, verify evidence, approve or reject creators, onboard profiles, and oversee the creator directory. | Request platform passwords, hold funds, or create payment records. |

## Acceptance criteria

A release is accepted when an unapproved creator application is not public; approval requires the admin review path; an approved profile has its own slug and catalog; an authenticated viewer can submit one active request per creator/game; public status contains no viewer identity; a creator can confirm ownership and transition the request; a viewer can suggest a new title; duplicate suggestions are not created; and role boundaries reject unauthorized procedures. All visible copy must preserve the no-funds boundary and avoid promises of purchase or stream timing.

## Trust, moderation, and reporting

The system treats viewer handles and notes as private creator-facing data, exposes only safe request status publicly, and keeps application checks, reviewer notes, and status events in the admin workflow. Reports, abuse review, and any off-platform dispute are handled by the administrator; the site does not adjudicate platform payments or promise recovery. External links are submitted as public URLs and are structurally validated without claiming ownership.

## Launch and revenue direction

The staged launch should first validate creator application completion, approved-profile discovery, request conversion, duplicate prevention, and creator response time with a small test cohort. Later, revenue can come from creator workspace subscriptions, operational tools, analytics, sponsored discovery placements, or a TikTok partnership package, provided those models do not require the site to hold viewer money or execute platform transactions. Partnership evidence should be measurable: active approved creators, request-to-response rate, repeat viewers, completed streams reported by creators, and content/viewership lift.
