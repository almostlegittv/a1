# AlmostLegitTV — Visual Direction

## Three possible approaches

### Theme Name: Signal Raid
**Very Brief Intro:** A tactile late-night stream-control-room aesthetic: sharp broadcast panels, scanline texture, radio labels, and an Xbox-green signal cutting through near-black. It feels personal, live, and creator-led rather than like a storefront.
**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Campfire Cartridge
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, editorial gaming-journal direction with parchment neutrals, archival game-card details, and a lived-in Sunday-stream mood. It gives the brand a friendly, low-pressure personality.
**Probability:** 0.04

### Theme Name: Arcade Field Notes
**Very Brief Intro:** A high-contrast field-guide approach with deep cobalt, orange ink, utilitarian stamps, and compact game dossier cards. It treats each suggested game as an episode waiting to happen.
**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen approach: Signal Raid

### Design Movement
**Signal Raid** draws on editorial broadcast graphics, analog signal interfaces, and contemporary gaming stream overlays. It avoids a generic neon-cyberpunk treatment by using luminous acid green sparingly as a live-status signal against graphite, bone, and concrete-gray surfaces.

### Core Principles
1. **The stream is the product.** The site frames each game as a future show, not as a retail item.
2. **Energy through structure.** Asymmetrical layout, number-like markers, and horizontal signal rails create movement without visual clutter.
3. **Proof over hype.** Clear status chips and direct explanations make game gifting, booking, and tipping understandable at a glance.
4. **Personal, not corporate.** Copy should sound like a real creator speaking directly to the community.

### Color Philosophy
Near-black graphite provides a focused stage that keeps media and calls to action prominent. Acid green is reserved for live, gifted, and action states; ember orange signals streamed or in-progress moments. Bone-white text prevents the site from relying on glare, while dim gray keeps supporting information quiet.

### Layout Paradigm
The page follows a **broadcast sequence** rather than stacked marketing blocks: a left-aligned channel masthead, an offset live-program hero, a ticker-like support explanation, a sideways scrolling roster of games on mobile, and a clear two-lane support split. Content uses measured rails and shifting panel edges instead of a centered-card grid.

### Signature Elements
1. A compact circular **AL transmission mark** with a broken outer ring and signal slice.
2. **Signal rails** — thin horizontal lines, timestamps, and channel labels that divide sections.
3. **Game dossiers** with status chips: Wishlist, Gifted, and Streamed / Planned.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should clarify a next step. Game cards expand to reveal exactly where a supporter leaves the site to purchase through Xbox/Microsoft. Status filters and mobile navigation use direct, low-latency transitions. Buttons feel like broadcast controls: firm, rectangular, and immediate.

### Animation
Use 160–240 ms transform-and-opacity transitions with a sharp ease-out. Sections may enter with a subtle upward slide and staggered timing; the live dot may pulse gently. Never loop large movement or simulate excessive visual noise. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
**Space Grotesk** provides the compact, technical display voice for headlines, labels, and game titles; **DM Sans** carries all body copy for relaxed reading on phones. Headlines are tight, mostly lowercase/uppercase mixed for rhythm; labels are mono-spaced, widely tracked, and small. Avoid Inter.

### Brand Essence
**AlmostLegitTV turns community game gifts into the next stream worth showing up for.** Personality: candid, high-energy, community-minded.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are short and energetic; CTAs are explicit about what happens next; microcopy answers trust questions before they are asked. Example lines: “Gift a game. Lock in the next run.” and “Buy through Xbox — the stream happens here.” Generic filler is banned.

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark pairs a broken-ring AL transmission icon with tightly tracked **AlmostLegitTV** lettering. The mark is designed to remain recognizable in a small social avatar or favicon, with no reliance on tiny text.

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Green — #B8FF2C.**

## Style Decisions

- Every major section uses a visible broadcast artifact: a channel rail with a broken-ring signal mark, a queue label, or a status marker.
- The game queue leads with a stream dossier—queue position, stream signal, status, and creator note—before its external Xbox purchase path.
- Calls to action name the real next move plainly, including the off-site Xbox purchase and private code handoff to the creator.
